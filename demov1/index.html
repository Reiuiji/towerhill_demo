Meet Tower Hill

“Bring your dancing shoes, your drinking hat, and an extra pair of socks, because Tower Hill will rock you out of yours!”

Lead Vocals & Frontwoman

Caroline Driscoll

A vivacious and powerful frontwoman, Caroline can be found singing her heart out from table tops, chairs, and the tops of bars most nights. In your face and interacting with the crowd, this quick witted dynamo keeps you dancing, singing, and laughing all night long.

Bass & Booking Coordinator

Bob Fallon

A staple of the south shore, bassist Bob Fallon is a natural performer and keeps the energy high all night. His passion and love of rock and roll literally drips from his luscious locks. Bob also handles booking and band coordination.

Lead Guitar

Jay Driscoll

This virtuoso guitarist brings the rock each and every night. His blistering solos and matinee idol good looks melt faces, and hearts.

Drums & Percussion

Stormy Thorson

Drummer Stormy Thorson combines performance and musicianship in every beat. A long time fixture on the local music scene, he is a charming and delightful character.
