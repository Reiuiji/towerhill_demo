Meet Tower Hill

"Bring your dancing shoes, your drinking hat, and an extra pair of socks, because Tower Hill will rock you out of yours!"

Lead Vocals & Frontwoman

Caroline Driscoll

A vivacious and powerful frontwoman, Caroline can be found singing her heart out from table tops, chairs, and the tops of bars most nights. In your face and interacting with the crowd, this quick witted dynamo keeps you dancing, singing, and laughing all night long.

Bass & Booking Coordinator

Bob Fallon

A staple of the south shore, bassist Bob Fallon is a natural performer and keeps the energy high all night. His passion and love of rock and roll literally drips from his luscious locks. Bob also handles booking and band coordination.

Lead Guitar

Jay Driscoll

This virtuoso guitarist brings the rock each and every night. His blistering solos and matinee idol good looks melt faces, and hearts.

Drums & Percussion

Stormy Thorson

Drummer Stormy Thorson combines performance and musicianship in every beat. A long time fixture on the local music scene, he is a charming and delightful character.

💡 Design Decisions & Solutions for the Band

1. Navigation Bar with Schedule, Center Logo & Menu Overlay

Implemented your exact idea: Top bar has [ 🗓️ Shows ] on the left, the real Black & White Logo showcased in the middle, and [ ☰ MENU ] with both icon and text on the right. When tapped, a full slide-over panel drops down with giant touch-friendly cards and Bob's phone number.

2. Built for the "Tech-Illiterate" (Older Fans & Casual Visitors)

No tiny icons without labels. High contrast colors, 48px+ touch targets, instant "Next Show" banner right under the hero, and a Font Size Scaler (A- / Normal / A+) so anyone with older eyes can read comfortably. Plus a floating sticky bar on phones with [ 📞 Call Bob ] [ 📅 Next Show ] [ 🎵 Listen ].

3. Event List instead of Clumsy Google Calendar

The original Wix site had a broken Google Calendar connector. We replaced it with a beautiful, readable Event List with huge date badges, filters (All / Public / Private), 1-click Google Maps directions, and 1-click "Add to Phone Calendar" (.ics export).

4. Music Listening Experience

Interactive audio player with soundwave visualizer and cover track playlist (Journey, Bon Jovi, Guns N' Roses, Adele, Van Morrison).

5. Contact Bob Kept Front & Center

Prominent Bob Fallon booking card with 1-tap call to (508) 326-4460 and 1-tap email to TowerHillRocks@gmail.com, plus a simple 3-field request form.

6. Social Media & Real Photos

Embedded Facebook & Instagram post cards, direct social follow links, and real show photos downloaded from their official gallery.
